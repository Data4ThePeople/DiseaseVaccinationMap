In 2026, through November 7, states reported 5,073 measles cases, the most in any year since 1991. How does your state's kindergarten vaccination rate compare?

Today we published a free, interactive map from guest contributor Liam Aranda-Michel, an OSV fellow. I met Liam at an offsite, we started talking, and I immediately asked him to start working on a project for us. This map is his vision, brought to life through Data 4 The People's process.

The map shows:
• reported cases of measles, whooping cough, hepatitis A, mumps and polio in every state and DC, from as early as 1920 through 2026
• the share of kindergartners with each vaccine, from the 2009-10 school year on
• each state's counties, with county vaccination rates and 2025-2026 county measles cases

Liam and I are not experts on measles. So, as part of our research process, we connected with Dr. Michael Kinch, who helped us enhance the visualization and validate the data. His essay on this year's outbreak is also on our site today.

Find your state at the link in the first comment.
