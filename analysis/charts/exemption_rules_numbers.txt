Kindergarten MMR coverage, last school year before the rule change and after

California: ended personal belief exemptions, first school year 2016-17. SB 277, signed June 30, 2015; applied to school entry from July 1, 2016. Source: https://www.leginfo.ca.gov/pub/15-16/bill/sen/sb_0251-0300/sb_277_bill_20150630_chaptered.htm
  2015-16 (last year before): 94.5%   2016-17: 97.3%   2025-26 (latest): 95.7%   change, last year before to latest: +1.2 points
  school years not a count of all schools: none

New York: ended its religious exemption, first school year 2019-20. Signed June 13, 2019, in effect at once. Source: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2019/S2994
  2018-19 (last year before): 97.2%   2019-20: 98.6%   2025-26 (latest): 97.7%   change, last year before to latest: +0.5 points
  school years not a count of all schools: 2020-21, 2022-23, 2024-25, 2025-26

Maine: ended non-medical exemptions, first school year 2021-22. Public Law 2019, ch. 154 (LD 798); upheld by voters in March 2020; in effect September 1, 2021. Source: https://legislature.maine.gov/legis/bills/bills_129th/chapters/PUBLIC154.asp
  2020-21 (last year before): 94.3%   2021-22: 96.7%   2025-26 (latest): 98.0%   change, last year before to latest: +3.7 points
  school years not a count of all schools: 2009-10, 2013-14, 2014-15, 2016-17, 2020-21

Connecticut: ended its religious exemption, first school year 2022-23. Public Act 21-6, signed and in effect April 28, 2021; K-12 students who already had one kept it; preschoolers who had one had until September 1, 2022, so 2022-23 is the first kindergarten class fully under the rule. Source: https://www.cga.ct.gov/2021/ACT/PA/PDF/2021PA-00006-R00HB-06423-PA.PDF
  2021-22 (last year before): 95.7%   2022-23: 97.3%   2025-26 (latest): 98.6%   change, last year before to latest: +2.9 points
  school years not a count of all schools: none

Mississippi: added a religious exemption, first school year 2023-24. Federal court order, April 2023 (Bosarge v. Edney); exemption offered from July 15, 2023. Source: https://msdh.ms.gov/Page/resources/19957.pdf
  2022-23 (last year before): 98.4%   2023-24: 97.5%   2025-26 (latest): 97.0%   change, last year before to latest: -1.4 points
  school years not a count of all schools: none
